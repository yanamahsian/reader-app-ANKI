# Russian translation rights audit: Proust / Flaubert / Kierkegaard

Purpose: curated candidate list for AN.KI Germany/EU distribution. A translation is treated as a separate copyrighted work; do not infer EU public-domain status from the original author's death alone.

## IMPORTABLE / term-expired candidates

### Marcel Proust — Adrian Antonovich Frankovsky (1888–1942)

Frankovsky died in 1942, so his translations are outside the ordinary German/EU life+70 term. Russian Wikisource exposes and labels as public-domain Russian texts including:
- `В сторону Свана` / `Du côté de chez Swann` — translation published 1927.
- `Германт` / `Le Côté de Guermantes`.
- `Пленница` / `La Prisonnière`.

Source family: Russian Wikisource. These are strong candidates for curated Wikisource ingestion.

### Gustave Flaubert

High-confidence old Russian translations:
- `Госпожа Бовари` — anonymous Russian translation, 1858. Anonymous publication is far outside the 70-year publication term.
- `Саламбо` — Nikolai Minsky (1855–1937). Translator term expired.
- `Иродиада` — Ivan Turgenev (1818–1883). Translator term expired.
- `Легенда о св. Юлиане Милостивом` — Ivan Turgenev (1818–1883). Translator term expired.

Russian Wikisource also exposes other Flaubert translations, but those should remain VERIFY_TRANSLATOR until the exact translator identity/death date is recorded in the audit registry.

Do NOT use Nikolai Lyubimov's `Госпожа Бовари` as public domain for Germany/EU: Lyubimov died in 1992, so the ordinary life+70 term runs through 2062 and public-domain status begins 1 Jan 2063.

### Søren Kierkegaard

High-confidence old Russian translations:
- `Афоризмы эстетика` — Peter (Petr) Hansen / П. Г. Ганзен (1846–1930), translation published 1886. Translator term expired.
- Old Hansen material from `Или — или` / `Наслаждение и долг` — eligible where the specific text is demonstrably Hansen's translation.
- `Несчастнейший` — Jurgis Baltrušaitis / Юргис Балтрушайтис (1873–1944), first Russian translation published 1908. Translator term expired.

Source family: Russian Wikisource.

## BLOCKED / modern Russian translations

### Proust — Nikolai Mikhailovich Lyubimov (1912–1992)
Lyubimov's Proust translations are still protected in Germany/EU under the ordinary life+70 rule through 2062; public domain from 1 Jan 2063. Do not ingest them without a licence even when a library or website offers free reading access.

### General rule
A modern reprint of an old public-domain translation does not by itself make the underlying old translation protected again, but new editorial contributions, annotations, scholarly editions, typography/scans, and database/site rights can have separate protection. Prefer clean public-domain source transcriptions with explicit provenance.
